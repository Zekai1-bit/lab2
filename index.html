Before opening

A Day with the Bookshop Cat

Come spend a day with Kitty, one scroll at a time.

Scroll down

9:05 AM

Morning

The shop opens at nine. Kitty opens at 9:05.

Stop by the little bookstall on the corner at nine and you'll hear the shutters rattle up. Wait five more minutes and you'll see Kitty stroll in, as if the shop had been waiting for him. Which, of course, it had.

Watch him inspect the new arrivals the way a critic reviews a novel: slowly, and mostly by sitting on them. Today's stack of puzzle books gets his verdict: sunny, firm, and perfect for watching customers like you.

2:30 PM

Afternoon

Two cats, one lap, zero interest in the plot.

By afternoon the sun has moved to the park across the street, and so has Kitty. Sit down on the grass with a paperback and a soft blanket, and you won't be alone for long: his friend Marmalade claims your lap first.

Try to turn a page. Marmalade leans on it. Then Kitty climbs aboard and pushes his head into your hand until you give up reading and start petting.

7:45 PM

Evening

Some stories are best read with your eyes closed.

When the streetlights flicker on, Kitty follows you home. He finds the book you left open on the bed and lies down beside it, just to see how the story ends.

Watch his eyelids grow heavier with every line. Let him sleep; you can finish the chapter tomorrow. The book will still be there in the morning. So will he.

The End

Thanks for keeping him company today. Goodnight, Kitty.

Author: Zekai Wan
